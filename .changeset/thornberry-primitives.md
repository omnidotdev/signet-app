Dialogs, confirmations, menus, cards, avatars, and tables now use the shared Omni components, so they match the rest of Omni: dialogs keep a gutter on phones and scroll when tall, menu items show a pointer, and long menus scroll instead of clipping.

Press `?` anywhere, or pick "Keyboard shortcuts" in the command palette, to see every keyboard shortcut.
